## A Character-by-Character Breakdown of the Party

### Reid Hershel — The Reluctant Swordsman

Reid is the entry point for almost everyone who plays. He begins as an ordinary young hunter living a quiet life in an Inferian village, and the story's inciting incident drags him into something far larger than he ever planned. In combat he is the front line: a sword user with reliable, easy-to-chain physical attacks and enough durability to stay in the thick of things.

For newcomers, Reid is the safest character to control while you learn the rhythm of the battle system. His moveset teaches you how combo timing, blocking, and positioning work without punishing small mistakes. Community reports from the original release often describe him as the "training wheels" hero — and that is a compliment, not a criticism.

### Farah Oersted — The Fist-First Fighter

Farah is Reid's childhood friend and the party's martial artist. She fights unarmed, closing distance quickly and unleashing fast punch-and-kick strings that reward players who like constant pressure. Where Reid absorbs hits, Farah dodges around them, which makes her the more exciting option once you are comfortable with the controls.

Player experience suggests Farah is the most satisfying character to control in longer sessions because her attacks flow almost without pause. The trade-off is that she can be surrounded if you overcommit. Keep an eye on your spacing, and she becomes a combo machine.

### Keele Zeibel — The Scholar Who Reads the Battlefield

Keele is a young scholar who studies Craymels, the spirit-like entities that power magic in this world. He is the party's brain: the one who explains how the two worlds connect, translates ancient knowledge, and generally keeps everyone pointed in the right direction. In battle he stays at mid-to-long range, casting elemental spells that hit groups of enemies.

An actionable tip: spell access in Tales of Eternia depends on which Craymels your casters are equipped with. Give Keele spirits that cover the elements your melee fighters cannot handle, and you will rarely run into an enemy you cannot damage.

### Meredy — The Girl From the Sky

Meredy comes from Celestia, the world above, and her arrival is what sets the entire plot in motion. She is a formidable Craymel caster in her own right, and her connection to both worlds makes her the emotional and narrative anchor of the second half of the story. Her distinctive personality and outlook have charmed players since the original release.

Players who invest time in Meredy's magic are rewarded with some of the party's most impactful spells. Because she and Keele draw from the same Craymel system, you can build them as complementary casters rather than duplicates — one focused on raw damage, the other on coverage and support.

## Combat Roles and Party Composition

The remaster keeps the original's Aggressive Linear Motion Battle System, a real-time 2D system where you control one character while allies act on their own. That makes role balance more important than raw stats.

| Role | Character | Preferred Range | What They Contribute | Watch Out For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frontline damage | Reid | Close | Consistent physical damage, holds enemy attention | Limited elemental coverage |
| Speed fighter | Farah | Close | Fast combos, quick repositioning | Can be swarmed if you overextend |
| Elemental caster | Keele | Mid to long | Elemental spells, group damage, utility | Fragile when enemies close in |
| Story caster | Meredy | Mid to long | Heavy magic, key plot moments | Needs protection and setup time |

Here are three lineup ideas for different stages of the adventure:

| Situation | Suggested Core | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| Learning the battle system | Reid, Farah, Keele | Two durable melee fighters plus ranged support keeps early fights simple |
| Long boss battles | Reid, Meredy, Keele | Reid draws attention up close while both casters chip away from a distance |
| Exploration and grinding | Farah, Keele, Meredy | Fast movement and wide-area spells clear random encounters quickly |

A few practical habits make any composition stronger:

- **Rotate who you control.** Switching between melee and caster teaches you enemy patterns from multiple angles.
- **Keep casters out of the scrum.** If an enemy beelines past your fighters, reposition manually instead of hoping the AI handles it.
- **Mix Craymel elements.** Two casters with identical loadouts waste a slot you could spend covering a weakness.
- **Watch the skits.** These optional scenes flesh out relationships between party members and often hint at upcoming story turns.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Every Character

- **Start with Reid, then branch out.** His forgiving moveset is the best tutorial the game offers.
- **Give Farah the controller when you want speed.** Her attack strings feel dramatically faster than anyone else's.
- **Treat Keele and Meredy as specialists, not clones.** Assign different Craymels so each caster covers separate elements.
- **Use skits to learn the cast.** They add context that the main story scenes sometimes skip.
- **Revisit characters after major story beats.** Move sets and spell options expand as the journey continues, so a hero you ignored early may become essential later.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Tales of Eternia Remastered Characters

**Who are the main Tales of Eternia Remastered characters?**

The core party centers on Reid Hershel, Farah Oersted, and Keele Zeibel from the world of Inferia, joined by Meredy from Celestia. Additional allies join the group as the story unfolds, and the official site describes the game as featuring "a cast of unforgettable characters."

**Which character is best for beginners?**

Reid Hershel. His sword combos are straightforward, he has enough durability to survive mistakes, and his playstyle teaches the fundamentals of the real-time battle system before you move on to faster or more technical heroes.

**What is the difference between Keele and Meredy?**

Both are Craymel casters, but their origins shape how they play and how they fit the story. Keele is the Inferian scholar who explains the world's rules, while Meredy is the mysterious visitor from Celestia whose arrival drives the plot. Since spells come from equipped Craymels, you can build them as complementary rather than identical casters.

**Is the cast different from the original 2001 release?**

No new party members have been announced. The remaster focuses on updated visuals, enhanced playability, and quality-of-life improvements for the returning cast, so fans of the original will recognize every hero on the roster.
