## Character Breakdown: Strengths, Weaknesses, and Playstyles

### Reid Hershel: The Reliable Lead

Reid is the protagonist, a young hunter who grows from small-town kid into the backbone of the team. His stats are the definition of well-rounded: solid attack, solid defense, and enough speed to land combos without fighting the controls. He is the closest thing to a default answer for the Tales of Eternia Remastered best character.

What makes Reid a strong pick is forgiveness. Miss a timing window and you lose a little damage, not the fight. His sword artes mix single-target punishment with area coverage, and he scales into the final act without demanding a specific build. If you have never touched a Linear Motion Battle System before, start here.

**Best for:** beginners, players who want a consistent main, and anyone who prefers melee over menu management.

### Farah Oersted: Speed, Combos, and a Safety Net

Farah is Reid's childhood friend and a hand-to-hand fighter who trades reach for raw speed. Her attack strings are quick, her mobility is excellent, and she can slip behind enemies while your casters charge their spells. She is arguably the most fun character to control in a straight fight.

The trade-off is durability. She is squishier than Reid and rewards aggressive play, which can land you in trouble. Her support-flavored artes soften that risk considerably, making her a hybrid who keeps the party standing while still contributing steady damage. Players who like being in the thick of it should give her a long look.

**Best for:** aggressive players, combo enthusiasts, and anyone who wants extra healing without benching a damage dealer.

### Keele Zeibel: The Elemental Cannon

Keele is the party's scholar and dedicated offensive mage. He hits elemental weaknesses harder than anyone else, and his higher-tier spells can wipe whole groups of random encounters in a single cast. Against a boss with a known weakness, he is often the difference between a long fight and a short one.

The cost is fragility. Keele has low physical durability, his spells take time to cast, and he burns through MP faster than anyone else. You will spend more time managing his positioning and resources than you will with Reid or Farah. The payoff is enormous if you enjoy planning around enemy weaknesses.

**Best for:** players who enjoy resource management, elemental puzzle-solving, and big damage numbers.

### Meredy: Versatility Through Craymels

Meredy is the mysterious girl at the center of the story, and her Craymel-based magic gives her the widest elemental spread in the party. She is less specialized than Keele, but she covers more situations without forcing a party swap, and her utility options keep her relevant when pure damage is not the answer.

She is also the character most tied to the plot, so investing in her pays off narratively as well as mechanically. Her learning curve sits in the middle: the kit is flexible, but getting the most from it means understanding how Craymels shape her spell list.

**Best for:** players who want flexibility, elemental coverage, and a character who stays useful from start to finish.

The remaster keeps the same story and the same cast as the original, with remastered music and sound, cleaner cutscene portraits, and toggles that let you play closer to the original release if you prefer. It also adds quality-of-life tools that quietly change how you assemble a team.

| Feature | What It Does | Effect on Character Choice |

| High-Speed Mode | Speeds up battles and supports auto-battle | Makes leveling a benchwarmer painless |

| Instant retry | Restarts battles immediately | Encourages experimenting with fragile casters |

That means the Tales of Eternia Remastered best character is less locked to your opening pick than it used to be — you can course-correct mid-playthrough without a grind marathon.

## FAQ: Tales of Eternia Remastered Best Character Questions

**Who is the Tales of Eternia Remastered best character for beginners?**
Reid Hershel. His balanced stats, forgiving timing windows, and straightforward sword combos make him the easiest character to learn while still being strong in the final act.

**Can I change which character I control during the game?**
Yes. As in most Tales titles, you can switch the party member you control and let the AI handle anyone you would rather not manage. The remaster's instant retry option makes testing a new main low-risk.

**Does the remaster add new playable characters?**
No. It retains the same story and cast as the original, with graphical and quality-of-life improvements rather than roster changes.

**Is there a definitive best character overall?**
Player experience suggests the roster is well balanced, so the Tales of Eternia Remastered best character is the one that matches your style — Reid for comfort, Farah for speed, Keele for burst damage, and Meredy for flexibility.
