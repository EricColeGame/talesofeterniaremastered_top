## Why "flying" by GARNET CROW Still Hits

There's a specific texture to Japanese rock from that period that modern game soundtracks rarely replicate. Fans in the comment threads under Bandai Namco's post described it as a sound that carries "a unique nostalgia" — the kind of guitar tone and vocal phrasing that instantly dates a song to a specific era and makes that dating a feature rather than a flaw.

For a remaster, that's strategically smart. Nostalgia is the entire product. If you're going to remaster a game from that generation, leaning into the music that defined it is far more effective than commissioning a modern replacement that would feel anonymous by comparison.

The Tales of Eternia Remastered opening song also serves a practical purpose: it's the first thing players hear. An opening theme sets expectations for tone, pacing, and emotional register before a single battle begins. "flying" signals adventure with a wistful edge — which fits a story about two intertwined worlds, Inferia and Celestia, and a cast trying to save both.
